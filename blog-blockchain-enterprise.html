Blockchain Beyond Crypto: Enterprise Use Cases

Home / Blog / Blockchain

Blockchain is more than cryptocurrency. Enterprise blockchain offers real solutions to real business problems.

What is Enterprise Blockchain?

Unlike public cryptocurrencies, enterprise blockchain:

  • Requires permission to participate
  • Is more energy-efficient
  • Offers better privacy and control
  • Integrates with existing systems

Real Enterprise Use Cases

1. Supply Chain Tracking

Track products from origin to consumer. Belgian retailers use this for food traceability—essential for recalls and authenticity verification.

2. Digital Identity

Secure digital IDs for employees, customers, and partners. GDPR-compliant identity management.

3. Smart Contracts

Automate contract execution when conditions are met. Example: Automatic payment when delivery is confirmed.

4. Document Verification

Certificates, diplomas, and credentials can be verified instantly without central authority.

When NOT to Use Blockchain

  • When a simple database will do
  • For high-volume, low-value transactions
  • When speed is critical
